# AGNITIA-TERRA06

**TERRA** is a hybrid solar + wind generation forecasting platform for a virtual co-located plant (40 MW AC solar + 50 MW wind = 90 MW total) in the Dewas wind belt near Indore, Madhya Pradesh. It forecasts generation to avoid deviation charges, plans battery dispatch, and provides an actionable UI for grid operators.

## Hero Features
- **H1 Control Room**: Unified UI with combined bands, demand overlays, and a live trust ribbon.
- **H2 Dispatch Advisor**: Linear programming solver that minimizes fossil backup and curtailment.
- **H3 Trust Score**: Rank-correlated confidence scores to alert operators when the models are uncertain.
- **H4 What-if Simulator**: Interactive scenario planning for weather events or battery expansions.
- **H5 Deviation Shield**: Blocks optimization based on CERC DSM rules to minimize financial penalties.

## Results (Test Period — Held-Out 179 Days)
Evaluated with strict leak-free lead mapping (leads 1–24: 24 h-old forecast `fx1_`; leads 25–48: 48 h-old forecast `fx2_`). Six models are compared per source on the test split: `persistence`, `week_mean`, `physics`, `gbm` (tuned with Optuna), `ensemble` (served model: physics+gbm blend with conformal calibration), and `chronos2_zs` (Amazon Chronos-2 zero-shot foundation model with weather covariates, run on Kaggle 2× T4 GPUs; comparison-only benchmark).

Generated by the evaluation harness (`docs/accuracy-report.md`):

| Source | Capacity | Model | MAE | RMSE | nMAE | Skill vs Persistence | 80% Band Coverage (PICP80) | 90% Band Coverage (PICP90) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solar | 40 MW | Ensemble (served) | 1.57 MW | 3.20 MW | 3.9% | +27.8% | 91.8% (daylight: 84.8%) | 96.5% (daylight: 93.6%) |
| Solar | 40 MW | LightGBM (tuned) | 1.57 MW | 3.20 MW | 3.9% | +27.8% | 86.2% (daylight: 74.4%) | 91.6% (daylight: 84.5%) |
| Solar | 40 MW | Chronos-2 ZS (benchmark) | 1.68 MW | 3.48 MW | 4.2% | +22.5% | 89.4% (daylight: 80.3%) | 94.9% (daylight: 90.6%) |
| Wind | 50 MW | LightGBM (tuned) | 3.99 MW | 5.96 MW | 8.0% | +38.3% | 76.8%* | 88.1%* |
| Wind | 50 MW | Chronos-2 ZS (benchmark) | 4.05 MW | 5.94 MW | 8.1% | +37.4% | 74.6%* | 86.5%* |
| Wind | 50 MW | Ensemble (served) | 4.15 MW | 6.10 MW | 8.3% | +35.8% | 72.6%* | 86.1%* |

*\*Honesty notes on tuning, wind ensemble & coverage:*
1. **Tuning impact**: Optuna tuning on a temporal train hold-out improved solar ensemble MAE by ~2.3% (from 1.60 MW to 1.57 MW) and wind standalone GBM MAE by ~0.4% (from 4.01 MW to 3.99 MW, with 80% band coverage rising ~5.6 points from 72.7% to 76.8%).
2. **Wind ensemble vs standalone**: The wind ensemble got slightly worse on test (+1.5% MAE, from 4.09 MW to 4.15 MW) because blend weights were fitted on the dry winter validation window (`val_fit`) and do not transfer to the monsoon-heavy test window; standalone tuned LightGBM (3.99 MW) and Chronos-2 (4.05 MW) beat the ensemble on test, and Chronos-2 achieved the lowest wind RMSE overall (5.94 MW).
3. **Chronos-2 serving**: Chronos-2 is maintained as a comparison-only benchmark; serving it in live/replay pipelines is excluded to keep backend deployment lightweight without PyTorch or multi-GB checkpoints.
4. **Seasonal coverage shift**: Wind PICP80 (72.6% ensemble, 76.8% tuned GBM) and solar daylight PICP80 (84.8%) differ from nominal 80% because conformal calibration was fitted on dry winter data, whereas the test split is monsoon-heavy with higher volatility.
