# Brag Plan: Vidyut (TERRA)

## What is this app?
Vidyut is a hybrid solar + wind generation forecasting platform: hourly forecasts for the next 24-48 hours with an 80% range, a per-hour confidence score, alerts, battery/backup planning and deviation-charge estimates, for a virtual plant at a real site near Dewas.

## The angle
A quiet, premium product film that lets the real UI do the talking. One continuous camera move from the headline answer ("About 812 MWh expected over the next 48 hours. Plan for 504 MWh of backup.") down into the range and confidence strip, then a simulated click through the new Location flow. The honest note (virtual plant, real weather, tested on held-out days) is part of the pitch, not fine print.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Tomorrow's power, hour by hour." over 48 bars that grow like the app's forecast outline strip.

## Key moments
- The control room headline and stat strip (real screenshot, Dewas replay run).
- Camera push into the 48 h chart: median forecast with its 80% range, then the "Forecast confidence by hour" strip.
- Location flow: pick Pavagada, Run live forecast, the three-step progress, the live result with its amber "not validated here" banner.
- Proof: 28% (solar, daylight) and 36% (wind) less error than persistence on held-out days.

## Outro / punchline
Vidyut. Hybrid solar and wind forecasting. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).

## User flow worth showing
Entry: Location page, choose a site. Key action: Run live forecast (progress: Fetching live weather, Running the forecast models, Planning battery and backup). Result: live 48 h forecast, confidence strip, alerts.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film, light paper palette, one dark proof beat
- Interpretation: few scenes, long settled holds, restrained motion, real UI as the hero.

## Duration: 24.5s (scenes cut on music beats)

Vidyut forecasts hybrid solar and wind output hour by hour, with an 80% range and a confidence score for every hour. Real Open-Meteo weather, virtual plant.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9 (114.84 BPM)
- Music treatment: fade in 0.6s, volume ~0.45, fade out over the last 1.6s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Strong cues locked: 3.70 (product reveal), 8.44 (camera push), 10.54 (confidence strip). Beat grid used for the Location click sequence: 14.22 select, 15.28 run, 16.34 result.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS breathes a soft background glow. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched (soft impact on reveal, card slide on the push, clicks on the simulated clicks, bell on the logo).
- Restraint rule: nothing loud; no sound under the proof numbers except the music.
