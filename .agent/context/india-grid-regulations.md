# India Grid Regulations

- **Source**: CERC DSM Regulations 2024
- **Notes**: Wind and solar deviation charges are calculated as a percentage of the Contract Rate (CR) (PPA tariff). They are not fixed INR/kWh values. The tolerance band is 5% for solar/hybrid and 10% for wind, with the X-factor at 1.0 (Available Capacity) up to FY2026-27. Since the slabs require a specific project's CR, we must keep illustrative: true in config/dsm.yaml and keep the illustrative fixed rates for the demo.
